# MOD files

ProTracker modules embedded into the firmware as its playlist (see
`MOD_FILES` in `../CMakeLists.txt`; the D1 button steps through them).

These are not my work. They are Amiga-era tracker modules collected since
the 1990s, from demoscene releases and BBS/disk swapping, kept here only so
the project builds and plays out of the box, for personal and educational
use. No ownership is claimed, and nothing here is sold or licensed on.

**If you are an author or rights holder and want a file removed, open an
issue and it will be taken down.**

| `JOHN.MOD` | VCC+GIGICAR 1989 !! | unknown (same title tag as `2010.MOD`) | unknown |

Tracker author credits are taken from the modules themselves (song title
and sample-name text). Several modules also use samples from the
Soundtracker `ST-xx` sample disks (the `st-01:...` sample names), which
were themselves largely sampled from synthesizers and records.

Corrections and missing credits are welcome.
